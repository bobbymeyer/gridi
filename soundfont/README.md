# the bundled soundfont

GeneralUser GS 2.0.3, by S. Christian Collins.

<https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser>

261 instruments and 13 drum kits, General MIDI and Roland GS compatible, in
32MB. Gridi loads it on first run, so a patch makes the sound it was written
for without anything being installed.

`LICENSE.txt` is Chris's, unaltered. It allows use in software projects and
redistribution; it also asks that nobody link directly to his download files,
which is why this copy is here rather than fetched from his site.

If you would like to support his work: <https://buymeacoffee.com/schristiancollins>

Drop any other `.sf2` on the canvas to play through that instead.
